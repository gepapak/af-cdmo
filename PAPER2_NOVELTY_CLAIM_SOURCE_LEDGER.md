## Current verdict

Paper2 has a defensible theorem-led T-EMPR contribution after the completed v6
multi-zone experiment and v12 AF-CDMO structural benchmark: economically
derived complete quotients for declared operator-certificate rewrites, scoped
necessity results for common neural families, exact input-output structural
assurance, and real-market evidence that syntactic presentation can corrupt
predictions and operational alerts.

Its generic neural-architecture novelty is moderate, not groundbreaking. Its
market-certificate construction is narrow and plausibly distinct. The v10
matched-control campaign is complete. Raw analytic-residual controls beat CQDM on
the original syntax, but incur material prediction drift and higher
worst-presentation MAE under registered equivalent rewrites. CQDM reduces matched
worst-presentation MAE by 16.3--38.3% across geometry and chronology while keeping
rewrite drift at numerical zero. This isolates representation assurance as the
empirical contribution. Failure to beat analytic KKT reconstruction does not
invalidate the theorems; it limits the accuracy claim while leaving
market-governance claims intact. AF-CDMO v12 adds a ten-seed synthetic result:
exact invariance under six presentation families and modest clean matched-task
gains, while the absence of an operator-validated Nordic affine geometry still
precludes a real-market AF-CDMO superiority claim.

## Search boundary

The audit covered primary conference/journal papers and official Nordic market
methodology located through broad web search. It did not provide authenticated
coverage of every Scopus or Web of Science record, unpublished manuscript, patent,
or proprietary operator implementation. Novelty language must therefore remain
qualified.
